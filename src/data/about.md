# Intro

My research interests center on the statistical foundations of modern machine learning, particularly the development of rigorous and computationally scalable methodologies for inference and learning under complex, evolving data-generating mechanisms.

I am especially interested in understanding how statistical procedures and learning algorithms behave beyond classical assumptions, particularly in settings characterized by high dimensionality, temporal dependence, heterogeneity, and distributional shift. My interests span statistical inference, uncertainty quantification, causal learning, and probabilistic modeling, with an emphasis on establishing reliable and interpretable methods for prediction and decision-making.

Through interdisciplinary research, I seek to bridge statistical theory with modern machine learning, investigating methodological challenges that arise across diverse scientific and real-world settings. My broader ambition is to contribute to the development of statistically principled learning systems that remain robust, computationally efficient, and trustworthy under realistic conditions.

Beyond research, I maintain a keen interest in emerging technologies, engineering, and developments in computational science.
