# Intro

My research interests lie at the intersection of statistical inference, statistical machine learning, and computational statistics, with a particular emphasis on developing rigorous and scalable methodologies for complex, high-dimensional data.

<br /><br />

I am fascinated by the mathematical foundations of modern learning algorithms, particularly understanding why they work, when they remain reliable, and under what conditions their statistical guarantees hold. My interests encompass uncertainty quantification, causal inference, probabilistic modeling, and adaptive statistical learning, especially in settings characterized by temporal dependence, heterogeneity, and distributional shift.

<br /><br />

Through interdisciplinary research spanning financial econometrics, behavioral science, healthcare, and decision optimization, I have become increasingly interested in the methodological challenges underlying modern statistical learning. Rather than focusing exclusively on predictive performance, I seek to understand the theoretical properties, limitations, and computational behavior of statistical procedures under realistic data-generating mechanisms.

<br /><br />

My broader ambition is to bridge rigorous statistical theory with modern machine learning, contributing to the development of reliable, interpretable, and computationally efficient methodologies for inference and decision-making. Beyond research, I maintain an interest in emerging technologies, engineering, and computational science.
